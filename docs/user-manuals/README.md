# User manuals

A guide for everyone who works with the Realhive POS, one per role. Keep yours to hand: when you
are stuck on a screen, look up what you were trying to do in your manual.

**Inside the POS:** choose **Manual** in the menu (Alt+2), or the manual button on the dashboard,
to read your manual on screen.

**Start here if you are new:** [Getting started](getting-started.md) covers signing in, your
password, the menu and its keyboard shortcuts - things every role shares.

## A few things that are true for everyone

- **You see only what your role allows.** A menu entry, a tab or a button you do not see is not
  broken: your role does not include it. The system checks the same rules itself, so hiding a
  button is never the only protection.
- **You work only at the branches you are assigned to.** If a branch is missing from a list, ask
  your manager to assign you to it.
- **Nothing is deleted.** A mistake is corrected by voiding, reversing or cancelling it, with a
  reason, and the original stays on record. Don't worry that one wrong click erases history - it
  can't.
- **Money is shown in Kenya shillings (KES).** Amounts "without VAT" are marked as such; shelf
  prices include VAT unless a product says otherwise.
- **Roles can be tailored.** The administrator can change what a role may do, so your screens may
  differ slightly from this manual. When in doubt, ask whoever manages your account.
