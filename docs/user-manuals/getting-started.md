## Where you can use the POS

Only at work: the POS opens on the internet connection of a branch or of head office, and nowhere
else. At home, on mobile data or on public wifi the page does not open (the browser shows
**Forbidden**), or signing in answers **"The POS can only be used from a branch or head office
network."** That is on purpose - it is not a fault with your account.

If a whole branch suddenly sees that message, its internet connection has probably changed (a new
line, or the backup line taking over). Tell your manager; the administrator has the branch's new
address added.

## Registered devices

You can sign in only on the shop's own tills and computers - ones your manager has **registered**.
The sign-in page says which device you are on (**This device: Till 2, Westlands**), or that it is
not registered. On any other device - your own phone, say, even on the shop's wifi - signing in
answers **"This device is not registered for the POS."**

To register a device (your manager does this with you):

1. Your manager opens **Devices**, names the device and chooses **Register**. They are shown an
   eight-character **code**.
2. On the device, open the POS. On the sign-in page choose **Register it** (or go to **Register
   this device**).
3. Type the code - capitals, spaces and the dash don't matter - and choose **Register device**.
4. **Sign in** as usual.

A code works **once** and for **30 minutes**. The device stays registered until your manager
revokes it - or until the browser's data is cleared, after which it needs registering again.
**Menu → Account** also shows which device you are on.

## Your first sign-in

Your manager or the administrator creates your account and gives you a **temporary password**.

1. Open the POS in the browser and enter your email and the temporary password.
2. You are taken straight to **Change password**. Type the temporary password as your current
   password, then your own new password twice. It must be **at least 12 characters**.
3. You are signed out. Sign in again with your new password.

You cannot use anything else until you have chosen your own password.

## Signing in every day

Enter your email and password on the **Sign in** page. Where you land depends on your role:
cashiers go straight to the **Till**, managers to the **Dashboard**.

You stay signed in while you work. If your password is changed, your roles change, or an
administrator signs you out, you will be asked to sign in again - that is expected.

## If you forget your password

1. On the **Sign in** page, choose **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter the email you sign in with and choose **Send the link**.
3. Open the password reset email and follow its link. The link **works once** and
   **expires after 30 minutes**.
4. On **Set a new password**, type your new password twice and choose **Set password**.
5. Choose **Sign in** and use the new password.

The page says the same thing whether or not the email has an account - that is on purpose, so
nobody can use it to find out who works here.

**No email?** Check the spam folder. Still nothing, or the link says it is no longer valid? Ask
for a new one, or ask your manager to use **Force a password reset** on your user page - that
signs you out everywhere and sends you a fresh link.

## Changing your password

Menu → **Account** → **Change password**. Enter your current password, then the new one twice.
Every device you are signed in on is signed out, including this one; sign in again with the new
password.

## Finding your way around

The menu along the top shows only what your role can use. Every entry has a keyboard shortcut:
hold **Alt** and press the key. Hover over an entry to see its shortcut.

| Alt + | Goes to | Alt + | Goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| T | Till | K | Cash |
| D | Dashboard | M | Customers |
| G | Reports | U | Users |
| J | Products | O | Roles |
| Q | Catalog setup | B | Branches |
| W | Pricing | Z | Settings |
| S | Stock | I | Audit |
| H | Purchasing | A | Account |
| 1 | Expenses | N | Suppliers |
| 2 | Manual | 3 | Devices |

On the till, **Alt+C, E, F, L, P, R, V, X and Y** belong to the till itself - see the
[cashier manual](cashier.md#keyboard-shortcuts).

**Lists and filters.** Most lists have filters at the top (branch, dates, category, a search box)
and a **Show** button. Long lists are split into pages with **Previous** and **Next**.

## Reading your manual in the POS

Every manual is in the POS itself, so you never need to hunt for a copy.

- **From the menu:** choose **Manual** (Alt+2). It is there for everyone, on the till too. The page
  shows **Your manual** - the one written for your role, or one for each role if you have several -
  with **Getting started** beside it. Manuals for the other roles are listed underneath.
- **From the dashboard:** if you see the dashboard, the button at its top right opens your
  manual directly (for example **Branch manager manual**).

**Download PDF** (or **PDF** on the list) saves a manual as a PDF to print or keep offline. A
printed copy says the date it was printed; the one in the POS is always the latest.

Links between manuals work as they do here, and **All manuals** takes you back to the list. If
your role has no manual of its own, start with Getting started and read the one closest to your
work.

## Your supervisor PIN

If you approve things at the tills - price changes, voids, refunds, cash moving to and from the
intraday - you need a PIN.

1. Menu → **Account** → **Supervisor PIN**.
2. Choose 4 to 6 digits and confirm with your password. Obvious PINs (1111, 1234, runs) are refused.
3. **Save PIN.**

At a till, the cashier's screen lists who can approve; you choose yourself and type your PIN. The
PIN approves **one action, at one branch**, and nothing else. Five wrong tries lock it for 15
minutes - your sign-in is not affected.

Never tell anyone your PIN. Approving with it is the same as signing for the action; it is recorded
in your name.

## When you are locked out

- **Wrong password several times:** the account locks for a short while - a minute at first,
  longer if it keeps happening (up to an hour). Wait, then try again carefully, or use **Forgot
  your password?**
- **Wrong PIN five times:** the PIN is locked for 15 minutes. Another supervisor can approve in
  the meantime.
- **"Your session has ended. Please sign in again."** Your password or roles changed, or you were
  signed out by an administrator. Sign in again.
- **Your account is suspended or deactivated:** only whoever manages users can restore it.
- **"This device is not registered for the POS."** Sign in on one of the shop's tills or computers,
  or ask your manager to register this one - see [Registered devices](#registered-devices).
- **"The POS can only be used from a branch or head office network."** You are not on a work
  connection - see [Where you can use the POS](#where-you-can-use-the-pos).

## Creating your own account

The sign-in page offers **Create an account**: you enter your name, email and a password, then the
code emailed to you. An account made this way has **no role** - it can sign in and see its own
**Account** page, nothing more - until your manager or the administrator gives it a role and a
branch. Staff are usually created by their manager instead (see the
[branch manager manual](branch-manager.md#adding-a-member-of-staff)).
